Our Legacy

Redefining Legal Excellence

Founded by James A. Talbert in 1984, Bates & Gates began with a revolutionary idea: that a law firm could combine the rigor of a white-shoe practice with the agility and innovation of a modern enterprise. Four decades later, that vision has grown into one of the nation's most respected litigation and corporate advisory firms.

Today, we stand at the forefront of legal innovation, representing Fortune 500 companies, emerging growth businesses, and high-net-worth individuals in their most complex legal matters. Our team of 40+ attorneys brings together former federal prosecutors, SEC officials, and partners from the world's leading law firms.

What sets us apart is our relentless commitment to strategic thinking. We don't just react to legal challenges—we anticipate them, developing proactive strategies that protect our clients' interests and advance their objectives.

Our Journey

Historical Milestones

Key moments that shaped our firm's legacy over four decades.

1984

Founded in Florida

James Talbert opens the firm's first office in Tampa with a vision for innovative legal advocacy and a team of five attorneys.

1995

First Major Victory

Landmark $500M settlement in complex commercial litigation establishes the firm as a powerhouse in corporate disputes.

2003

Expansion to Washington D.C.

Opens D.C. office to better serve clients navigating federal regulatory matters and government investigations.

2010

Chambers Tier 1 Ranking

Recognized as a top-tier firm for Litigation and Corporate Law, cementing our reputation among the nation's elite.

2018

Global Reach

Opens San Francisco office, establishing a truly national platform for technology and emerging growth clients.

2024

40th Anniversary

Celebrating four decades of excellence with 40+ attorneys, $3.2B recovered, and recognition as a Legal Innovation Award winner.
